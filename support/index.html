Last updated: July 25, 2026

For help with Liltmoon, email wangtuz29@gmail.com.

When contacting support, include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not include payment-card details, passwords, Apple Account credentials, or other sensitive personal information.

Subscription help

  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account subscription settings.
  • Use the app's Restore Purchases action after signing in with the Apple Account used for purchase.
  • Refund requests are handled by Apple through its purchase support process.
