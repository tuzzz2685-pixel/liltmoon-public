Purchases & subscriptions

购买与订阅（中文摘要见下段要点；详细以英文结构为准，App 内也可查看订阅页说明。）

Manage or cancel

  • Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions.
  • Select Liltmoon to change plan, turn off auto-renew, or cancel. Changes follow Apple’s rules and the terms shown at purchase.
  • Apple’s guide: View, change, or cancel subscriptions.

Restore purchases

  • Membership stays with the Apple Account that paid—not with a Liltmoon login (there isn’t one).
  • After reinstall or a new device, open Liltmoon → Profile (or the membership screen) → use Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.
  • If premium still does not appear: check network, confirm the Apple Account, wait a moment, then try Restore again. Storefront or family-sharing rules are controlled by Apple.

Trial, renewal, and billing

  • Trial length, price, and renewal text shown by the App Store at purchase are the source of truth.
  • If a trial ends and you did not cancel, Apple may start paid renewal according to those terms.
  • Liltmoon does not charge card numbers directly and does not see full payment-card details.

Refunds

  • Refunds are handled by Apple, not inside Liltmoon chat.
  • Start at reportaproblem.apple.com or Apple’s purchase support, using the Apple Account that bought the subscription.

中文要点：管理/取消请到 设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅。会员跟 Apple Account 走；重装后用同一账号在 App 内「恢复购买」。退款请走 Apple 官方渠道。试用与续费以购买时系统展示为准。

Data on this iPhone (reinstall / new device)

本机数据与重装换机

  • Night Map, notes, Check-ins, and path preferences are stored on this iPhone by default. They are not a Liltmoon cloud backup today.
  • Reinstalling the app or moving to a new iPhone does not automatically transfer that local night history. There is currently no account-based restore for Night Map or journals.
  • Membership is separate: it follows your Apple Account. Use Restore Purchases after reinstall (see above). Restoring membership does not restore deleted or lost local night records.
  • Clear local data (Profile → This iPhone) removes local history and preferences on this device only. It does not cancel App Store membership.
  • Optional anonymous feature counts (only in eligible Internal QA builds, off by default) never include notes, identity, or exact event times. Standard releases keep that collection off.

In the app you can also open Profile → Privacy & Help → How your data works for the same boundaries in the device language.

中文要点：夜间记录默认只在这台 iPhone。重装或换机不会自动带走 Night Map/笔记；目前也没有云端换机恢复。会员用「恢复购买」找回权益，但不等于找回本机历史。「清除本机数据」只清本机，不取消订阅。

Feedback & contact

The preferred way to share product feedback is the in-app Send Feedback form (Profile → Privacy & Help). You can choose a type, write a message, and optionally leave an email for a reply. Submissions go through Formspree to the Liltmoon inbox after the service confirms receipt.

Email alternative: wangtuz29@gmail.com. For a technical issue, include app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description.

Do not include payment-card details, passwords, Apple Account credentials, private journal entries, or sensitive health information. Liltmoon is not an emergency or medical support service.

Privacy requests

For a privacy question or to request deletion of feedback or support correspondence, email wangtuz29@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy request”.
