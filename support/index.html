Liltmoon Support

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Feedback and help

The preferred way to share product feedback is the in-app Send Feedback form. It lets you choose a feedback type, write a message, and optionally leave an email address for a reply. The form is delivered through Formspree and is accepted by the app only after the service confirms receipt.

Email is available as an alternative: wangtuz29@gmail.com. For a technical issue, include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Do not include payment-card details, passwords, Apple Account credentials, private journal entries, or sensitive health information in the form or email. Liltmoon is not an emergency or medical support service.

Privacy requests

For a privacy question or to request deletion of feedback or support correspondence, email wangtuz29@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy request”.

Subscription help

  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account subscription settings.
  • Use the app's Restore Purchases action after signing in with the Apple Account used for purchase.
  • Refund requests are handled by Apple through its purchase support process.
