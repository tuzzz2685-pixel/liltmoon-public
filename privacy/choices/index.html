Liltmoon Privacy Choices

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Anonymous usage summaries

Standard releases keep product analytics disabled. In an eligible Internal QA build, Share anonymous usage summaries is off by default. You can turn it on under Profile > This iPhone. Collection begins only after you turn it on and does not import earlier night history.

You can turn the switch off at any time. This stops future collection and removes summaries waiting to upload. It does not delete or change your on-device Night Map, notes, check-ins, profile preferences, or App Store membership.

Clear local data

Clear local data removes local app information and resets analytics consent and pending analytics state. Removing Liltmoon may also remove information stored locally on your iPhone. Neither action cancels an App Store subscription.

Summaries already combined into group totals

Accepted Internal QA summaries are immediately combined into group-level weekly totals. Liltmoon does not retain raw report, user, device, session, or event tables. Because there is no account or lasting identifier that can locate one person's contribution, Liltmoon cannot retrieve or delete an individual contribution after it has been aggregated. Group totals expire after no more than 26 weeks.
