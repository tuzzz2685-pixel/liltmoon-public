Liltmoon Privacy Choices

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Anonymous usage summaries

Standard production releases keep product analytics disabled. In an eligible Internal QA build, “Share anonymous usage summaries” is off by default. You may turn it on under Profile → This iPhone. Collection begins only after you turn it on and does not import earlier nighttime history.

You may turn the switch off at any time. Turning it off stops future collection and removes summaries waiting to upload. Your local Night Map, journal, Check-ins, profile preferences, and App Store subscription are not deleted or changed.

Clear local data

The in-app clear-local-data action resets analytics consent and pending analytics state together with local app data. Removing the app may also remove information stored locally on the device.

Already aggregated summaries

Accepted Internal QA summaries are immediately combined into group-level weekly cells. Liltmoon does not retain a raw-report, user, device, session, or event table. Because there is no account or lasting identifier that can locate one person's contribution, Liltmoon cannot retrieve or delete an individual contribution after it has been aggregated. Group aggregates expire after no more than 26 weeks.
